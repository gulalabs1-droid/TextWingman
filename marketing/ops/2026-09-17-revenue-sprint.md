# Text Wingman Revenue Sprint

## Objective

Get the first verified paid customer by turning qualified attention into measured product use. The success ladder is:

`10 real testers -> 20 reply sessions -> 5 free accounts -> 2 checkout starts -> 1 paid customer`

Do not treat views or followers as the primary outcome.

## Product flow

1. Send every visitor to `/tiktok`, not a generic social profile link.
2. Use one promise everywhere: `Paste the text they sent. Get 3 natural replies in 10 seconds. Free.`
3. Let the visitor see the first useful reply without creating an account.
4. After the result, ask them to `Save this thread + build your Style DNA`.
5. Keep the thread in the restore handoff so signup never makes the visitor start over.
6. Only judge pricing after real users reach checkout.

## Campaign link format

Every post needs a unique `video_id` and `utm_content`:

`https://gula-agents2.vercel.app/tiktok?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=reply_test_2026_09&utm_content=tw-###&video_id=tw-###`

Use the same `video_id` on YouTube and Instagram for the same creative. Change only `utm_source` by platform.

## Seven-day distribution test

- Publish two controlled posts per day per platform: one morning and one early evening Eastern Time.
- Use the same six-creative cohort across TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram.
- Use concrete first-frame problems: `maybe`, left on read, dry `lol`, `we need to talk`, too intense, and a last-minute cancellation.
- Show the exact text, the bad reply, the better reply, the Gula result, and one CTA: `Paste yours in the free tool in bio.`
- Use Fastlane for human reaction openings and HeyGen for clear explanation/reply reveals.
- Respond to every genuine comment the same day. Turn useful comments into the next reply video.

## Tester outreach

Recruit ten people directly before making another large batch. Send this individually, not as a mass blast:

> I’m testing a free texting coach that reads a real message and gives three replies that sound natural. Paste one real text, try one reply, and tell me which option you would actually send. It takes two minutes: [tracked link]

Ask one follow-up question: `What stopped you from creating a free account after seeing the reply?` Log each person in the Growth OS lead queue.

## Daily review

At 24 and 72 hours, record views, stayed-to-watch, profile visits, bio clicks, landing sessions, composer starts, people with a reply, copies, signup CTA clicks, signups, checkout starts, and paid users.

- Views but no profile visits: change the first frame and profile promise.
- Profile visits but no site sessions: repair native bio links and CTA wording.
- Site sessions but no composer starts: simplify the landing page and lead with the paste box.
- Replies but no signup CTA clicks: strengthen the Style DNA/save value proposition.
- Checkout starts but no purchases: only then test price, trial terms, or checkout trust.

## Anti-noise rules

- Never buy followers or use fake engagement.
- Exclude owner, audit, tester, and crawler traffic from decisions.
- Do not create a new batch until the current cohort has enough 24/72-hour data.
- Do not call a creative a winner from views alone; require profile intent, site starts, or replies.
