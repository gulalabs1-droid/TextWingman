## Operating conclusions

1. YouTube is the only platform currently demonstrating repeatable discovery at scale. Its winning pattern is concrete text conflict with a clear social consequence, not generic AI branding.
2. TikTok is getting tested in For You and Search, but no one is moving from view to profile. More uploads alone will not fix that leak.
3. Instagram is reaching new people but has almost no profile intent. The account is not flagged, so the issue is creative/profile conversion rather than a visible restriction.
4. Keep the existing two-slot schedule through Sep 7. Do not add a larger batch until the profile/link leak and the comment response loop are repaired.
5. Prioritize three creative families next: exact-text consequence, bad reply versus better reply, and live/reaction rewrite. Reuse the winning situations from YouTube and the selfie/Bumble search terms from TikTok.
